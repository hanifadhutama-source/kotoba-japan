# KOTOBA VISUAL DESIGN

## Overall Direction

Modern Tokyo-inspired educational adventure game.

The visual style should feel:

- Modern
- Clean
- Youthful
- Playful
- Slightly cinematic
- Japanese
- Approachable
- Suitable for an Indonesian vocational student project

The game should NOT look like a generic anime game.

It should feel like a stylized 3D Tokyo environment.

---

# WORLD

## Main Setting

Tokyo, Japan.

The world should be a compact playable Tokyo hub rather than a complete recreation of Tokyo.

The player should feel like they are exploring a small part of Tokyo.

---

# LOCATIONS

The world should gradually contain:

1. Tokyo Station-inspired area
2. Japanese school
3. Convenience store / Konbini
4. Ramen shop
5. Japanese residential street
6. Small park
7. Shrine area
8. Urban shopping street
9. Tokyo-inspired landmark for the Final Quest

---

# VISUAL ELEMENTS

Use recognizable Japanese environmental elements:

- Japanese road signs
- Train station signage
- Convenience store signs
- Vending machines
- Japanese storefronts
- Torii gate
- Small shrine
- Utility poles
- Japanese-style rooftops
- Pedestrian crossings
- Neon signs
- Street lamps
- Small urban shops
- Bicycle parking
- Japanese vending machines

Use these elements carefully.

Do not overcrowd the environment.

---

# COLOR DIRECTION

Primary:
- Japanese red
- White
- Charcoal / black

Secondary:
- Warm beige
- Soft gray
- Subtle gold

Accent:
- Small amounts of Tokyo-style neon colors

Do NOT make the entire game red.

The environment should remain visually balanced.

---

# UI

UI should feel modern and minimal.

Use:
- clean panels
- subtle glassmorphism
- Japanese-inspired red accent
- clear typography
- strong hierarchy

Japanese text should be easy to read.

Use a Japanese-compatible font such as Noto Sans JP where appropriate.

---

# NPCs

Keep the existing voxel/block character style.

Do NOT replace the entire character system.

Characters should feel like:
- students
- shopkeepers
- teachers
- commuters
- tourists
- residents

---

# AVOID

Do NOT use:
- excessive anime styling
- excessive kawaii styling
- excessive cherry blossom decoration
- fantasy RPG architecture
- giant random torii gates everywhere
- excessive Japanese flags
- generic stock-photo Japan aesthetic
- overly saturated colors

Tokyo should feel like a believable stylized game environment.

---

# DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Every visual element should support one of three purposes:

1. Exploration
2. Japanese learning
3. Story / atmosphere
